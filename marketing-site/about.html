About Us

Empowering Maryland's small businesses since 2020

Established in 2020, AL TAX SERVICE is a full-service Tax, Accounting, and Corporate Services firm dedicated to empowering individuals, entrepreneurs, and small business owners throughout the Maryland region. We specialize in providing reliable, personalized financial and business solutions that help our clients navigate the complexities of the U.S. tax system and maintain compliance with ever-changing federal, state, and local regulations.

Our mission is to simplify tax and financial management, allowing small-business owners to focus on what they do best — growing their businesses. From tax preparation and strategic tax planning to bookkeeping, payroll, business formation, and corporate compliance, we offer comprehensive services tailored to meet the unique needs of each client.

Who prepares your return

AL TAX SERVICE, 1714 Saint Paul St. STE 1A, Baltimore, MD 21202, +1 (443) 835-4492. We do not hold ourselves out as a CPA firm or a law firm, and we do not give legal advice. Under Maryland law (Business Occupations and Professions Article, section 21-403), an individual tax preparer who is registered with the State Board of Individual Tax Preparers and is not a certified public accountant, enrolled agent, or tax attorney must disclose to the customer in writing: their name, address, and telephone number; that they are not a CPA, enrolled agent, or tax attorney; the services they are qualified to provide; and their education and training, including examinations taken and passed. We give each client that written disclosure for the person preparing their return, and you may ask for it at any time. Only a person identified to you as a CPA, enrolled agent, or attorney holds that credential.
