# Fifth pass — everything outside the Document Builder (2026-10-15)

Scope: the firm's service agreements and every other client-facing document or text that the earlier passes did not touch. Four reviewers read primary text (Maryland statutes from mgaleg.maryland.gov, eCFR, IRS revenue procedures and forms) and edited disjoint files; their findings were checked by the owner's assistant where they carry a legal consequence.

## Service agreements (src/modules/contracts/contractContent.ts)
- Late charge: the old 1.5%/month interest plus attorney's-fee clause applied to everyone. Now a 1.5%/month **late fee under Commercial Law §14-1315** for **Business Clients only** (starts 30 days after the invoice, never on earlier late fees). Individuals pay no late fee. §14-1315(f) caps a consumer-contract late fee at 1.5%/month with 10-point bold disclosure and no charge before 15 days.
- Work product: a client's own records and a copy of any return the firm signs are always handed over (Treas. Reg. §1.6107-1(a); Circular 230 §10.28 for practitioners; Pub. 1345). Other unfinished work may be held, never past a legal deadline.
- Liability cap, indemnity, jury waiver, claims period: Business Clients only; claims period mutual 2 years; carve-outs for fraud, intentional misconduct and gross negligence (BJ's Wholesale v. Rosen; Ceccone v. Carroll Home Services).
- E-signature: separate, optional, conspicuous consent (Maryland UETA, Commercial Law §21-104(b)(3); 15 USC §7001(c)). The public sign page and public contract page now have two checkboxes.
- Reporting agent: Rev. Proc. 2012-32 §5.05 requires a written statement at signing and at least quarterly. The quarterly statement is now sent by `src/common/reportingAgentStatement.ts` (cron, first week of Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct). Clients who cannot be emailed are written to the audit log for a paper copy.
- Scopes rewritten to match what clients actually receive (payroll: 941/940/944, W-2/1099, MD withholding, MD UI, EFTPS; sales tax: annual report/personal property return; notice work needs a signed 2848 and a named attorney/CPA/EA). Arabic edition added to every scope; the PDF cannot draw Arabic, so a "Print English + Arabic" button opens the HTML copy.
- **Immigration**: Commercial Law §14-3301(e)(2) defines "legal services" to include completing and filing forms; §14-3303(1) bars an immigration consultant from providing legal services; §14-3304 requires a bilingual written contract with two exact 12-point statements, a 72-hour right to rescind and itemized fees; §14-3305 requires a posted sign; §14-3306 makes a violation a misdemeanor (up to $1,000 / 1 year) plus refund and attorney's fees. The contract now allows only secretarial services (typing, copying, translating into English). Staff must not complete or submit immigration applications.
- Individual Tax Preparers Act (BOP §21-403(b)): written disclosure before work begins and on the website; violation is a Title 13 unfair practice, $500 per violation (§21-404). Built as Document Builder form `tax_preparer_disclosure`. The Title 21 sunset date is July 1, 2027 (§21-502). The website carries a general paragraph; per-preparer details are intentionally not published for now (owner decision 2026-10-15).
