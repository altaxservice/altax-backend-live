# Limited Power of Attorney — Licenses, Permits & State Tax Filings

Source: the firm's own template `Templates/New Client Docs/POA - TEMPLATE.docx` (client R&Z Convenience and Tobacco LLC), rebuilt in the Document Builder as `limited_poa_filings` (English + Arabic).

## What was kept from the firm's template
Principal = the client business; Attorney-in-Fact = the firm, represented by two named people; limited scope (sales and use tax license, Articles, EBT/SNAP permit, filing sales/use and withholding tax, occupancy permit, signing the related papers); effective on signing; ends when the Principal revokes by written notice; documents/funds returned on termination; Maryland law; separate acceptance by each attorney-in-fact.

## What was improved
- Powers are individual switches (added: tobacco license, health/food permit, local licenses, SDAT annual report/personal property return, unemployment insurance, free-text other).
- Names who signs for the business (name + title) and has them confirm authority — the original had a blank "Principal's Printed Name" only.
- Added an explicit **Limits** clause (no sale/mortgage, borrowing, bank accounts, checks, ownership change, gifts), a **Duties** clause (records, copies of filings, confidentiality), optional end date, agent resignation, copies/reliance, and an optional **notary acknowledgment**.
- First-person wording ("my Attorney-in-Fact") replaced with consistent third-person defined terms; "extract" → "obtain".
