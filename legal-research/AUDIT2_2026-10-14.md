# Document Builder — second hard audit (50 documents, English + Arabic)

Method: seven independent reviewers, each assigned a group of documents, who read the template source and both language renderings, checked Maryland and federal rules against primary sources where reachable (mgaleg.maryland.gov, irs.gov, ecfr.gov, labor.maryland.gov, dhcd.maryland.gov and others), and staged exact replacement entries; every change was applied by script and re-tested (markup check on all 50 documents, a wording scanner over 300 renderings, and 300 PDF/Word/HTML exports with 0 failures). This is research and drafting work, **not legal advice**. Many statute pages were read as tool summaries, not verbatim; items that still need an attorney are listed below and in each document's "review note".

## Cross-cutting improvements
- **Automatic mistake checks** (`src/modules/docBuilder/checks.ts`): saving is blocked, and a "Please fix before saving" list is shown, for ownership/profit percentages that do not total 100, an end date before the start date, a home security deposit above one month's rent or a home late fee above 5% (RP §8-203, §8-208), a deposit larger than the price, a refund larger than what was paid, no power selected on a power of attorney, and similar. Tested (`src/tests/docChecks.test.ts`), including a test that every check points at a real question.
- **Optional notary acknowledgment** for any document that does not already have one: a last step before Review adds one acknowledgment page per signer (English and Arabic), with the county filled in.
- **Free-text "Additional terms"** added to the agreements that lacked it; notices, assignment, counterparts/e-signature, no-waiver and further-assurances boilerplate where missing.
- Arabic mirrored for every change; `scripts/docAudit/artifacts.ts` scans all renderings for doubled punctuation, empty brackets and repeated words.

## Most important findings and fixes (by group)
**Client letters and agreements.** Authorization to release information now carries the IRC §7216 / Rev. Proc. 2013-14 statements. Fee agreement default late interest cut from 1.5% to 0.5% a month (Maryland 6% legal / 8% written-loan rates), records clause follows 31 CFR §10.28, client duties, file retention. Payment plan and demand letter: consumer-debt switch and the Maryland collection-conduct protections; warnings about reviving old consumer debts.

**Powers of attorney and family.** Estates and Trusts Title 17 defines the principal as an individual and carves out entity filings and government forms (§17-101, §17-109(b)), so the Business and Limited POAs are most likely outside Title 17 — copy and reliance wording is now a request and agreement, not a statutory rule. Affidavit uses the exact Rule 1-304 sentences. Personal POA: real-property sales must be executed like a deed and recorded (RP §4-107); agent cannot sign an LLC's governing documents; self-dealing limits, no delegation, principal's acknowledgment, and optional specific property/account, family-support, trust, estate, charity, professionals and borrowing powers (added from a user-supplied sample). Gift letter: interested-party statement, donor account trace. Child travel consent: the review note no longer implies US border law requires a parental letter.

**Entities.** S-corporation second-class-of-stock traps removed (liquidation strictly by ownership percentage; profit-sharing percentages must equal share ownership); member agency under §4A-401; Maryland six-month withdrawal default (§4A-605) replaced by the agreement's own rule; coherent buyout timetable (election, price, appraiser, payment, acceleration); partnership valuation and dissolution rewritten; duty-of-care and loyalty limits aligned with §9A-103; partnership-representative and imputed-underpayment clauses; ownership transfer given a defined Transfer Date and an outside date.

**Sales and transfers.** Bulk-transfer trap fixed: notice to the Comptroller and creditors must go out at least 10 days before the buyer takes possession **or pays** (Commercial Law §6-105, §6-107; Tax-General §§11-505, 13-802), so no deposit is released and no possession taken until the notice period ends; non-resident seller withholding rewritten per Tax-General §10-912; Real Property §14-117 notices (Critical Area, transfer tax, military noise, homestead) and condo/HOA cancellation rights; sales-agreement implied-warranty disclaimer now conspicuous and consumer sales handled (Commercial Law §2-316, §2-316.1); termination documents handle refund, return of property, undoing licenses/leases/notices.

**Leases.** Residential entry clause updated to RP §8-221 (written notice of at least 24 hours stating date, time and purpose; Monday–Saturday 7 a.m.–7 p.m. unless the tenant agrees); late fee capped on the unpaid portion; deposit receipt lists the tenant's rights; assistance-animal and no-self-help wording; optional lead-form agent acknowledgment; commercial lease abatement contradiction fixed and the memorandum-of-lease rule corrected (RP §3-101: first term or any one renewal term over seven years); sublease ends with the master lease; amendment asks whether it is a home or a business lease.

**Lending and services.** Late charges now follow Commercial Law §12-105(c)(3) (after 15 days, up to the greater of $2 or 5% of the late payment); full 18 U.S.C. §1833(b) whistleblower notice in the NDA and consulting agreement; service and consulting late interest default 0.5% a month; real assignment of deliverables with a license-back; 1099-NEC threshold $2,000 for payments in 2026 and later; personal guaranty limited to the obligation described, optional revocation; vehicle security wording corrected.

**Employment.** Non-compete pay-level question (150% of the state minimum wage, L&E §3-716) now always asked and required; contractor backup withholding; Form W-4 is no longer described as a condition of employment (only the I-9 is), Maryland MW507 requested; payday stated (L&E §3-504); sick and safe leave paid/unpaid by employer size; incident report records the date the business learned of the injury and the MOSH reporting deadlines; warning letter anti-retaliation wording; verification letter tense and privacy fixes.

## Still UNVERIFIED — take to a Maryland attorney
- **Powers of attorney:** whether the Business/Limited POAs are outside Title 17; good-faith-reliance wording for a non-statutory form; whether an agent may also be a witness; whether SDAT, county/health offices and tobacco/lottery/alcohol/SNAP offices accept an agent's signature; medical-consent delegation to a non-parent.
- **Entities:** the LLC distribution-limit wording; enforceability of "lower of outside offer or appraisal" buyout prices and goodwill exclusion, especially for a removed member and for S-corporation buy-sell prices (Treas. Reg. §1.1361-1(l)); BBA partnership-audit clauses (IRC §§6221(b), 6223, 6225); SDAT filings on a transfer; license change-of-ownership rules; spouse-consent pages (practical, not a waiver of divorce-court powers).
- **Sales:** Comptroller bulk-sale and equipment sales-tax practice; tobacco and alcohol license transfers, SNAP/EBT ownership change, Lottery timing (only COMAR 36.02.03.15 was read: a Lottery license is not transferable); deposit forfeiture as agreed damages; exact statutory condo/HOA notice wording (staff must attach it); homestead web address; flood-risk disclosure; non-resident withholding rates and forms.
- **Leases:** the RP §8-221 effective date and emergency wording; whether the 60-day notice (RP §8-402) applies to commercial month-to-month tenancies and fixed-term expiry; lead-paint exemptions and re-distribution cycle; commercial late fee stacked with interest; whether silence leaves a statutory distress right; monthly pet fees; $35 returned-payment guidance; smoke/CO alarm wording.
- **Lending:** whether an LLC is a "corporation" for the no-limit exemption in Commercial Law §12-103(e), and whether an owner-to-company loan is a "commercial loan"; whether Title 12 limits reach interest on unpaid invoices; §12-114 consequences; lender licensing; collection attorney's-fee limits in consumer credit; limitation periods and "(SEAL)"; UCC filing location; enforceability of a guarantor's advance waiver of defenses; FTC Notice to Cosigner wording.
- **Employment:** enforceability of customer non-solicit and staff no-hire clauses against workers at or below 150% of minimum wage; whether continued employment is consideration for an existing employee's non-compete; Maryland unemployment-insurance separation notice and continuation coverage; the new-hire reporting deadline and MW507 use; OSHA Form 301 timing and recordkeeping thresholds; Healthy Working Families Act notice timing; contractor work-for-hire versus assignment.
- **Client letters:** whether §12-103 limits late interest on a service invoice for an individual; the TIGTA email address written from memory in the §7216 consent; whether an open-ended consent duration is acceptable for §7216; foreign-recipient and e-signature consent rules; whether a signed payment plan restarts the limitation period on a business debt.

## Decisions left for the attorney (deliberately not included)
Liability caps, shortened limitation periods or arbitration in the fee agreement; "(SEAL)" on notes and guaranties; a residual-knowledge clause, non-solicitation or jury waiver in the NDA; pet rent and 10+ unit air-conditioning rules in the residential lease; gift-of-equity wording; confession-of-judgment clauses (omitted on purpose); distress, forcible re-entry, fixed liquidated damages and receivers in commercial leases.
